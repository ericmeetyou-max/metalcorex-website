How 3d printing works

Inside the manufacturing process.

A typical sequence is shown below. The final route is adapted to your part, selected material and acceptance criteria.

01

Check the digital model

Review the geometry, minimum features and intended use. Select the process and material around the properties the prototype needs.

02

Orient & prepare the build

Choose the build direction and support strategy where applicable. Orientation can affect appearance, strength and dimensional behavior.

03

Build layer by layer

Produce the part from the prepared model. The layer-forming method depends on the additive process selected for the project.

04

Remove & clean

Separate the part from the build and remove supports or residual material as applicable. Access to enclosed features matters at this stage.

05

Post-process as required

Cure resin parts where needed, or apply the agreed smoothing, machining or cosmetic treatment. The route depends on the material and process.

06

Review fit & function

Check the prototype against its intended purpose. Record design feedback before the next iteration or a transition to production tooling.

3D Printing FAQs

Talk to our team ↗

Will a printed prototype behave like a molded part?+

Not necessarily. The material and build process can change strength, surface finish and thermal behavior. Choose the prototype route around the test you need to perform.

Which file should I provide?+

Send a STEP model when available, or a suitable mesh file such as STL, together with units and a drawing of critical dimensions.

Can printing help before tooling?+

A physical model can support geometry, access and assembly reviews before tooling. It does not replace the validation required for the final production material and process.
