How die casting works

Inside the manufacturing process.

A typical sequence is shown below. The final route is adapted to your part, selected material and acceptance criteria.

01

Review alloy & geometry

Evaluate wall transitions, draft, ribs and the parting line. Identify sealing faces, machined interfaces and required mechanical properties.

02

Develop the casting die

Plan filling, venting and ejection. Tool trials help assess the casting geometry and the features needing refinement.

03

Fill the die

Inject molten alloy into the reusable die under pressure. The process parameters depend on the alloy, tooling and component.

04

Cool, eject & trim

Allow the casting to solidify, then remove it from the tool. Trim runners and overflow material and remove flash as specified.

05

Machine critical features

Finish bores, threads and mating faces that need a machining operation. Account for the condition and location of the cast stock.

06

Finish & verify

Review dimensions and surface condition to the agreed criteria. Specify leak testing or other special verification when your application requires it.

Questions, answered

Die Casting FAQs

Need help with a specific drawing?
Talk to our team ↗

Can a casting include machined threads and faces?+

Yes, secondary machining can be part of the proposed route. Mark the relevant features and tolerances on the drawing.

Is die casting the best choice for low volumes?+

Tooling cost is a key consideration. For small quantities, machined parts or a different process may be more suitable; the geometry and project requirements guide the choice.

What should I specify for a sealed housing?+

Provide the operating conditions, sealing interfaces and acceptance criteria. Any leak test and documentation need to be agreed during quotation.
