Understanding the process

Cast polymer parts without a full production mold.

Urethane casting commonly uses a master pattern and flexible mold to reproduce low-volume parts. Silicone components use a compatible mold and cure system. The route can support appearance and fit evaluations, but cast materials should not be assumed identical to the thermoplastics they resemble.

Material & project fit

Selected polyurethane casting resins or silicone systems.

Final grade, geometry, finish and acceptance criteria are confirmed for your project before production.
