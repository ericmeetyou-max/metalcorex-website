The final surface

Finish is part of the specification.

Appearance, protection and fit are considered together. Ask about compatibility with your material and selected process.

Anodizing

For compatible aluminum parts; define color, masking and dimensional allowances.

Powder coating

A coated surface for selected metals; specify texture, color and protected areas.

Bead blasting

Creates a more uniform matte appearance; define cosmetic and functional faces.

Brushing & polishing

Directional or polished surfaces; align on a reference finish and visible areas.

Specify the complete surface

More than a color.

Describe texture, gloss, visible faces and any reference sample. Identify threads, electrical contact areas or mating surfaces that must remain uncoated.

Include in your material specification

  • Grade, temper or condition
  • Application and operating environment
  • Finish type, color and texture
  • Masking and post-finish dimensions
  • Required material or inspection records
