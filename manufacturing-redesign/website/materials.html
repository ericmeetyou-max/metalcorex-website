Materials & finishes

The material is
part of the design.

Match strength, weight, environment, appearance and manufacturing requirements to the application. Final grades and finishes are confirmed during project review.

01 / Metals

Metal components

Aluminum, stainless steel, steel, brass and copper. Specify the grade and condition needed for your application.

Common discussion points: corrosion, weight, threads, conductivity, finish and mating surfaces.

02 / Plastics

Engineering plastics

ABS, PC, PA, POM and PP, with other material requirements reviewed against the selected process.

Common discussion points: temperature, impact, wear, appearance and dimensional stability.

03 / Finishing

The final surface

Anodizing, powder coating, painting, polishing, brushing, plating, bead blasting and laser marking.

Define color, texture, visible surfaces and areas that must remain uncoated. Compatibility depends on the material and process.
