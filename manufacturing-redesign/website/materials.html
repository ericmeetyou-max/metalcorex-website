How to use these material references

These technical profiles link to grade sheets, mill catalogues or manufacturer selection guides, as labelled. They support preliminary selection and are not certificates for supplied material. Branded polymer examples do not represent every resin in the family or guarantee brand availability. Properties depend on stock form, thickness, treatment and test conditions. Request the exact supplier or compound sheet for production approval.

Regional grades are not automatic equivalents. Specify the required GB/T, ASTM, EN or JIS standard and edition. Confirm material records, compliance requirements and any substitutions in the quotation.

Common questions

Before you choose.

Can I request a material or finish not listed?

Yes. Include the exact designation or manufacturer datasheet in your RFQ. We review custom grades, specialty coatings and combinations against the drawing.

Are similarly named grades interchangeable?

No. Regional alloy names, resin brands, tempers and material conditions can differ. Any substitution should be agreed explicitly before production.

Can one part have multiple finishes?

Yes. Identify the zones and processing order on the drawing. Masking, transitions and coating buildup need to be considered for each surface.

How do I specify a molded texture?

Use an SPI grade, VDI number or a specific texture reference. Review draft and resin behavior, and use an approved sample for cosmetic acceptance.

Are certification and compliance requirements included automatically?

Include the required documentation and standards in your enquiry. Material suitability, traceability and any application-specific test requirements are confirmed in the quotation.
