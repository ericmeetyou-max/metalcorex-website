    Before you send

    A few useful details.

    Can I enquire without a CAD model?

    Yes. Describe the component and share any available drawing, sketch or reference image. We’ll clarify what is needed to assess manufacturability and prepare a quote.

    Can I request prices for different quantities?

    Yes. Enter the quantities you want quoted, such as 100, 500 and 1,000 pieces. Add the annual forecast separately if available.

    What should I include for a finished assembly?

    Include part drawings, a bill of materials where available, finish requirements and assembly instructions. Identify purchased hardware and any functional tests.

    How are delivery requirements handled?

    Include your destination and target timing. Shipping arrangements, lead time and delivery terms are agreed in the quotation.
