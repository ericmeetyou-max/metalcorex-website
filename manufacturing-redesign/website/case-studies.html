01 · Speaker Grille System

Balance acoustic opening, impact resistance, and cabinet fit.

A grille is not simply perforated sheet. Open area influences acoustic transparency; bridge width and formed depth influence stiffness; coating thickness and foam influence final fit.

Typical route
Perforation or laser cutting · forming · deburring · coating · foam assembly
Review inputs
Driver projection, cabinet opening, edge retention, foam stack, cosmetic face, service access

Manufacturing decision

Choose hole geometry and sheet gauge together, then validate formed stiffness before locking the coating and foam stack.

Inspection evidence

Hole pattern and pitch, overall profile, edge condition, coating appearance, fit fixture, and approved visual standard.

02 · Line Array Rigging Hardware

Protect the load path from drawing release through final finish.

Rigging components need a different review discipline from cosmetic enclosure parts. Material identity, load direction, hole position, edge condition, joining, heat treatment, and finish interfaces must be agreed before production.

Typical route
Certified stock · CNC or laser cutting · bending · controlled joining · finish · dimensional inspection
Review inputs
Load case, safety factor owner, mating pins, hole wear, bend orientation, critical dimensions, corrosion environment

Manufacturing decision

Separate safety-critical dimensions and material requirements from general cosmetic features on the drawing and inspection plan.

Inspection evidence

Material record, first-article dimensions, gauge or fixture result, finish record, and agreed sampling plan. Structural certification remains project-specific.

03 · Amplifier Thermal Housing

Treat heat flow, machining, finish, and assembly as one system.

An amplifier housing can combine an extrusion, machined thermal interfaces, cosmetic outer faces, threaded assembly points, connector cutouts, and electrically sensitive finish requirements.

Typical route
Extrusion or billet · CNC machining · deburring · cleaning · masking · anodizing · assembly check
Review inputs
Thermal interface flatness, PCB and connector datums, thread engagement, airflow, finish masking, cosmetic zones

Manufacturing decision

Define functional datums and thermal contact zones before applying cosmetic tolerances to the rest of the housing.

Inspection evidence

Datum-based dimensions, flatness where specified, thread gauges, finish sample, mask boundary, and trial assembly.

04 · Molded Horn & Functional Plastic

Preserve acoustic geometry while designing for molding and assembly.

Waveguides, horns, handles, and speaker frames combine visible surfaces with ribs, bosses, clips, draft, shrinkage, and fastening interfaces. The best time to resolve those conflicts is before mold steel is cut.

Typical route
DFM · material review · mold design · sampling · dimensional and appearance approval · production
Review inputs
Acoustic surfaces, wall transitions, draft, rib and boss design, gate and ejector zones, texture, insert or fastener strategy

Manufacturing decision

Protect acoustic and assembly-critical surfaces, while allowing tooling-led adjustments in hidden structural regions.

Inspection evidence

Approved sample, critical dimensions, texture and color standard, assembly fit, and controlled change record.
