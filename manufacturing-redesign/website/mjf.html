Understanding the process

Powder-bed polymer parts for complex geometry.

HP Multi Jet Fusion applies agents to a polymer powder layer and uses thermal energy to fuse selected regions. Loose surrounding powder supports the build. After cooling, parts are recovered and cleaned, with finishing selected for appearance or function.

Material & project fit

Supported polymer powders, commonly PA-based materials; exact grade is confirmed in the quote.

Final grade, geometry, finish and acceptance criteria are confirmed for your project before production.
