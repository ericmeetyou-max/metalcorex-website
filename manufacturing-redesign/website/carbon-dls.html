Understanding the process

Light-based printing with a material-specific cure.

Carbon Digital Light Synthesis uses projected light and an oxygen-permeable interface to form resin parts. Subsequent processing, including thermal curing for applicable materials, develops the intended material properties. Resin choice, orientation and the full finishing route are evaluated together.

Material & project fit

Supported Carbon resins; material availability and application suitability are confirmed per project.

Final grade, geometry, finish and acceptance criteria are confirmed for your project before production.
