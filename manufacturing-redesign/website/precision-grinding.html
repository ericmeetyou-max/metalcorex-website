Understanding the process

Finish critical geometry with an abrasive process.

Precision grinding removes small amounts of material with an abrasive wheel. It can refine dimensions, form and surface condition after earlier machining or heat treatment. The grinding method is chosen around the feature, material hardness and measurement requirement.

Material & project fit

Compatible ferrous and nonferrous materials, reviewed for hardness and geometry.

Final grade, geometry, finish and acceptance criteria are confirmed for your project before production.
