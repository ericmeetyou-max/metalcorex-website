Understanding the process

Form a material charge with heat and pressure.

Compression molding places a measured material charge into a mold and shapes it under pressure, often with heat. The material may flow and cure or cool depending on its system. Charge placement, flash control and demolding affect the final component.

Material & project fit

Compatible elastomer, thermoset or composite systems following application review.

Final grade, geometry, finish and acceptance criteria are confirmed for your project before production.
