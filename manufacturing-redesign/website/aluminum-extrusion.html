How aluminum extrusion works

Inside the manufacturing process.

A typical sequence is shown below. The final route is adapted to your part, selected material and acceptance criteria.

01

Define the cross-section

Review walls, channels and hollow features. Provide the overall envelope and specify functional dimensions and visible faces.

02

Prepare the die & billet

Develop the extrusion die and prepare the selected aluminum billet. Alloy and intended temper are reviewed for the application.

03

Extrude the profile

Push heated aluminum through the die to form the continuous cross-section. Geometry affects material flow and manufacturability.

04

Cool, straighten & age

Cool the profile and carry out straightening and heat treatment as appropriate to the specified alloy and temper.

05

Cut & machine

Cut the profile to the required length, then add holes, threads, end features or pockets that cannot be formed by extrusion alone.

06

Finish & inspect

Apply the agreed finish and verify length, key profile features and appearance. Protect visible surfaces for handling and shipment.

Questions, answered

Aluminum Extrusion FAQs

Need help with a specific drawing?
Talk to our team ↗

Can the profile include holes along its length?+

The die forms a continuous cross-section. Cross holes, localized pockets and end features generally require a secondary operation.

What is needed for a custom profile quote?+

Send a cross-section drawing, alloy and temper, cut length, quantity, critical dimensions and finishing requirements.

Can extrusion be used for a heat sink?+

Finned profiles are a common application. Thermal performance depends on the overall design and operating conditions, which should be supplied for engineering review.
