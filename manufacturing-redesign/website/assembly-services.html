How assembly works

Inside the manufacturing process.

A typical sequence is shown below. The final route is adapted to your part, selected material and acceptance criteria.

01

Align the assembly definition

Confirm the drawing revision, bill of materials and supplied components. Define which interfaces and functions must be checked.

02

Receive & kit components

Check incoming parts against the agreed requirements and group them by assembly. Resolve mismatches before they reach the workstation.

03

Join the components

Follow the specified sequence for fasteners, inserts or joining operations. Identify torque, adhesive or handling requirements where relevant.

04

Check fit during assembly

Review alignment, clearances and component orientation at the appropriate stages, before later operations hide the interfaces.

05

Verify the completed unit

Check completeness and agreed functional or visual criteria. Inspection fixtures and records are defined with the project scope.

06

Label, protect & pack

Apply the specified identification and arrange accessories or spare hardware. Choose packing that protects the assembled unit in transit.
