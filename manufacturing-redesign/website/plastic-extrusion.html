Understanding the process

Continuous profiles with a consistent section.

Plastic extrusion pushes softened polymer through a die to form a continuous cross-section. Cooling and sizing stabilize the profile before cutting or secondary work. It suits parts whose geometry remains largely constant along their length.

Material & project fit

Compatible thermoplastics such as selected PVC, PE, PP or other extrusion grades.

Final grade, geometry, finish and acceptance criteria are confirmed for your project before production.
