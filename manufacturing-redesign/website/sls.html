Understanding the process

Laser-sintered polymers with freedom of form.

SLS uses a laser to fuse selected areas of polymer powder. The surrounding powder supports the part during the build, enabling complex forms without separate support structures in many cases. Cooling and powder removal remain important parts of the manufacturing route.

Material & project fit

Suitable polymer powders, often nylon-based, selected by application.

Final grade, geometry, finish and acceptance criteria are confirmed for your project before production.
