Who we are

A manufacturing partner
for the whole component.

Xiamen Metalcorex Precision Technology Co., Ltd supports drawing-based custom manufacturing for metal and plastic parts.

We connect CNC machining, sheet metal fabrication, injection molding, casting, extrusion and additive manufacturing with finishing and assembly. The starting point is your design; the goal is a part that fits its application and its production requirements.

From a single machined interface to a multi-part enclosure, we consider material, geometry, surface treatment and assembly together. That gives your project a clear route from the first technical discussion to the agreed deliverable.

02 / A practical engineering conversation

Good manufacturing
starts before cutting.

We review the drawing, application and production requirements together so that the selected process supports the features that matter.

  • Geometry & manufacturabilityReview access, wall sections, bends, draft and features that affect the manufacturing route.
  • Material & finishAlign grade, condition, coating and cosmetic expectations with the intended environment.
  • Quantity & production stageConsider prototype, tooling and repeat-production requirements when defining the scope.
  • Acceptance & documentationAgree critical features, sample approval and required records before production.

05 / Working with Metalcorex

A clear path
from enquiry to delivery.

Know what needs to be agreed at each stage, with the technical details kept connected to your order.

  1. Share the design

    Send CAD files, drawings, material preferences, quantities and destination requirements.

  2. Review & quote

    Align the process, critical features, tooling, finish, inspection and commercial scope.

  3. Sample & approve

    Review prototypes or first articles where specified, then agree the production-release criteria.

  4. Produce & verify

    Manufacture and check parts against the approved revision and acceptance requirements.

  5. Pack & deliver

    Confirm identification, documentation and protective packaging for the agreed delivery arrangement.
