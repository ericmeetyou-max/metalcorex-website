Understanding the process

From a flat blank to a functional enclosure.

Sheet metal fabrication combines cutting, bending, hardware installation and joining. The part is developed from flat stock, so material thickness and bend geometry shape the design. A coordinated fabrication route keeps mounting holes, cosmetic surfaces and assembly interfaces working together.

Material & project fit

Aluminum, stainless steel and carbon steel sheet in a specified grade and thickness.

Final grade, geometry, finish and acceptance criteria are confirmed for your project before production.

How Sheet Metal Fabrication works.

A typical manufacturing route. The final sequence follows your design, material and inspection requirements.

01

Develop the flat pattern

Review thickness, bend directions, reliefs and hole locations.

02

Cut and deburr

Produce the blank and remove edge burrs before forming.

03

Form and join

Bend in a planned sequence, install hardware and weld where required.

04

Finish and inspect

Check the formed dimensions, fit and specified coating or surface treatment.
