How sheet metal works

Inside the manufacturing process.

A typical sequence is shown below. The final route is adapted to your part, selected material and acceptance criteria.

01

Develop the flat pattern

Review thickness, bend radii and the formed geometry. Define bend allowances and check whether holes or cutouts interfere with bends.

02

Cut the blank

Cut the required outline, slots and holes from sheet. Nesting and orientation are planned around the material and part geometry.

03

Deburr the edges

Remove cutting burrs and prepare edges for handling, bending and joining. Specify any edge conditions important to the application.

04

Form the bends

Use a planned bend sequence to create the three-dimensional shape. Tool access and springback are considered when setting up the part.

05

Join & add hardware

Weld components or fit specified inserts, studs and fasteners. Check their locations against the assembly interfaces.

06

Finish & inspect

Apply the agreed surface treatment and verify dimensions, alignment and cosmetic surfaces before protective packing.

Design for manufacturing

Small design decisions.
Real production impact.

These are useful starting points for a design review, not universal limits for every part.

Bend relief

Provide room around intersecting bends so material can form without tearing.

Hole-to-bend spacing

Keep holes and slots clear of the bend zone to reduce distortion.

Bend consistency

Consistent radii and practical flange lengths simplify tooling and setup.

Welded assemblies

Mark weld locations and define the cosmetic and distortion requirements.

Questions, answered

Sheet Metal Fabrication FAQs

Need help with a specific drawing?
Talk to our team ↗

Should I send a flat pattern or a formed model?+

A formed model plus a dimensioned drawing is a useful starting point. If you provide a flat pattern, include material, thickness and bend information for review.

Can hardware be fitted before delivery?+

Specify the hardware type, location and assembly drawing. Inserts, studs and fasteners are reviewed as part of the fabrication route.

How should I define visible surfaces?+

Mark cosmetic faces and provide the finish, color or reference sample. Also identify areas to keep free of coating.
