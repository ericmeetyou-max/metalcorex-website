Buyer Concerns

Common audio OEM requirements we design around.

Acoustic geometryWaveguides and horns require accurate surfaces, controlled tooling strategy, and stable batch reproduction.
Cosmetic finishAmplifier panels, knobs, grilles, and visible trims need finish samples and clear acceptance standards.
Outdoor durabilityPowder coating, anodizing, stainless hardware, silicone sealing, and packaging reduce field failures.
Assembly fitThreads, inserts, brackets, gaskets, and tolerance stack-up are reviewed before production release.
